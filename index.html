KNOW THE RULES

RAT SMP RULEBOOK

Read the rules carefully. Breaking them may result in punishment.

01

Hacking / Hacked Clients

Using hacked clients or cheating tools is prohibited.

Punishments: 7-day ban → 1-month ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
02

Swearing

Swearing is allowed, but directing it at other players is not.

Punishments: Warning → 5-minute mute → 10-minute mute → 1-hour mute → 1-day mute → 1-day ban → 7-day ban → 1-month ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
03

Slurs / Disrespectful Language

Slurs and disrespectful language are not permitted.

Punishments: 30-day ban → Permanent ban

UNAPPEALABLE
04

Toxicity

Do not engage in toxic behavior toward other players.

Punishments: Warning → 10-minute mute → 30-minute mute → 1-day mute → 1-day ban → 7-day ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
05

DDoS / Doxxing / Threats

DDoS attacks, doxxing, threats, and similar malicious behavior are strictly prohibited.

Punishment: IP ban from the server

UNAPPEALABLE
06

Spamming

Do not flood chat or repeatedly send unwanted messages.

Punishments: Warning → 5-minute mute → 10-minute mute → 1-hour mute → 1-day mute → 1-day ban → 7-day ban → 1-month ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
07

Advertising

Advertising other servers or unrelated services is prohibited.

Punishments: 1-day ban → 7-day ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
08

Raiding

Organizing or participating in raids against the server is prohibited.

Punishment: Permanent ban

UNAPPEALABLE
09

Alternate Accounts (Alts)

Alt accounts are allowed for farms only. Using alternate accounts to evade punishments is prohibited.

Punishment: A longer ban on the main account and a 1-month ban on the alt account. Depends on the severity of the main account's punishment.

APPEALABLE — DEPENDS
10

Real-Life Threats

Threatening another person in real life is strictly prohibited.

Punishment: Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
11

Sexual Content / Harassment

Sexual content and sexual harassment are prohibited.

Punishment: Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
12

Griefing

Do not destroy or damage other players' builds or property.

Punishments: 10-day ban → 20-day ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
13

Lying to Staff / Staff Impersonation

Do not intentionally deceive staff or pretend to be a member of the staff team.

Punishments: 7-day ban → 21-day ban → 1-month ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE
14

Severe Offenses

For serious violations not specifically covered by another rule.

Punishments: 7-day ban → 21-day ban → 1-month ban → Permanent ban

APPEALABLE — DEPENDS
